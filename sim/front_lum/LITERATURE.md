# Prior art for the angle-selective recycling OLED (survey 2026-10-29, from search snippets/abstracts; numbers UNVERIFIED)

Closest prior art
1. Kim, Qu, Coburn, Forrest, ACS Photonics 5, 3315 (2018), doi 10.1021/acsphotonics.8b00539 (+ patent US10770690):
   top-emitting OLED on a ~240 um PTFE diffuse reflector + Al, planarised with an n~1.8 polymer; green EQE 15 -> 37 % (~2.5x).
   No front angle filter; thick diffuser; pixel spread not assessed.  -> closest for the back-diffuser half.
2. Gao, George, Beeckman, Neyts, Opt. Express 28, 12837 (2020): DBR reflects light outside a cone back into a scattering
   phosphor (etendue reduction).  -> closest physical analogue of the whole concept (colour converter, not OLED).
3. Wankerl et al., Sci. Rep. 12, 5226 (2022): optimised multilayer on pc-LEDs for angular recycling.
4. Benisty, De Neve, Weisbuch, IEEE JQE 34, 1612 / 1632 (1998): metal/DBR cavities, photon recycling (theory).
5. LCD BEF + diffuse back reflector (3M, e.g. US5917664): on-axis gain ~1.6.
Other relevant: Sony microdisplay in-pixel MLA (2.2x forward); Joo et al. Science 370, 459 (2020) metasurface mirror;
Wu group (NTU) 3D reflective pixel / bank (Adv. Sci. 5, 1800467 (2018); Org. Electron. 2022, up to +30 % EQE);
Kim, Kim, Yoo, Nat. Commun. 16, 11606 (2025) in-pixel near-planar outcoupling, crosstalk-free;
US10586833 (Bragg filter on OLED for colour compensation); Schwab et al. Opt. Express 22, 7524 (2014) corrugated TE-OLED;
sputtered IZO on HATCN/MoO3 buffers (inverted, ACS AMI 2015); CaF2/ZnS DBR as encapsulation-compatible layer (ACS Omega 2022).
Apparently new: monolithic red-shifted angle-selective DBR on a transparent top electrode + thin (<= few um) internal
diffuser on Ag; on-axis current-efficiency framing (2.5-3x, orientation-insensitive); lateral-spread quantification with
reflective banks.  To check: OLED patents (Samsung/LGD/BOE/Sony) combining angle filter + scattering reflector.
